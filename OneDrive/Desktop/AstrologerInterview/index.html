To prepare an accurate birth chart (Kundli), we require three details: your Date of Birth, Exact Time of Birth, and City/Place of Birth. If you are booking for marriage compatibility, please provide details for both individuals.
What if I don't know my exact birth time?
If your birth time is approximate (within a 15–30 minute range), Hanish Bagga conducts birth-time rectification by verifying significant past life events (education, marriage, career shifts, major milestones) before delivering future predictions.
How does an online consultation work? Is it as effective as in-person?
Yes, completely! Astrological readings rely on mathematical planetary coordinates, not physical touch. Online consultations take place over private Zoom, Google Meet, or direct phone calls, allowing clients from across the globe to receive the same in-depth attention and recorded remedy summaries.
Will I be advised to perform expensive rituals or wear costly gems?
No. Hanish Bagga strongly believes in logic-driven, affordable, and practical remedies (Lal Kitab Upay, dietary adjustments, habitual timing alignments, and spatial energy rebalancing). No fear tactics or forced ritual expenditure are ever practiced.
Is my consultation strictly confidential?
100% confidential. Your birth charts, discussion notes, questions, and personal matters are never shared or published under any circumstance.
